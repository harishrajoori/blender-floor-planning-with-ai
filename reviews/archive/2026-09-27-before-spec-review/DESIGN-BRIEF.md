# Karimnagar Family Home — Owner Design Brief

**Status:** current owner requirements for concept design
**Selected site:** [Property 2 — southwest corner 54 × 66 ft](properties/PROPERTY-2-CORNER-WEST-SOUTH-54x66.md), owner-confirmed 27 September 2026.
**Canonical Vaastu research:** [knowledge/VAASTU-KNOWLEDGE.md](knowledge/VAASTU-KNOWLEDGE.md)

This brief is the human-readable source for any AI tool, architects, civil/structural engineers and the family's Vaastu consultant. It is not a sanctioned or construction plan.

## Goal

Create a strong owner-approved architecture concept that professionals can retain as far as practical while making necessary changes for survey, municipal rules, structure, services, safety and consultant-specific Vaastu.

## Households

- Open ground + two residential floors.
- Ground floor: garden, parking and flexible family-function space; no residence.
- First floor: brother and family.
- Second floor: owner and family.
- Independent entrances; no shared internal access.
- Shared stair and lift with separate household doors; either home must remain reachable while the other is locked.

## Ground floor

- Open stilt / outdoor garden / parking / temporary family-function use.
- Retain one car and two bikes as the current baseline; no four-car capacity assumption.
- Prefer larger usable east/northeast garden space, with a clear northern approach.
- Shared stair/lift access remains clear in both parking and function arrangements.
- A guest toilet or other enclosed service room is a design proposal, not a newly fixed owner requirement.

## Second floor - owner and family

- Two bedrooms plus one home office.
- Master sleeping zone in SW with attached bathroom outside the SW sleeping corner (no specific wardrobe/dressing room, use cupboards).
- Bedroom 2 toward W/NW.
- Office toward N/NW, accessible without exposing bedrooms.
- Open living–dining with practical furniture clearance, not a hotel lobby or long corridor.
- Separate kitchen in SE with compact utility and safe hob/sink/exhaust arrangement.
- Separate daily pooja and Mallanna pooja rooms in NE.
- Shared pooja preparation/lobby may serve both, but neither pooja room is a passage to the other.
- No bathroom shares a wall with either pooja room or directly faces its door.
- One common bathroom near Bedroom 2/office, reached from a short common lobby.
- Every private room has common-circulation access; no bedroom acts as a passage.

## First floor

No detailed room specification is owner-fixed. Default design direction: an independent, efficient 2BHK for the brother's family with living/dining, kitchen, common bathroom, master attached bath, optional pooja/quiet room and useful terrace.

Coordinate structure, kitchens, bathrooms and circulation with the owner's second floor. Keep pooja rooms separate from bathrooms, including vertical stacking. The earlier draft's duplicate office and two shrines on both floors are not owner-fixed requirements.

## Entrance and site use

Use the confirmed 54 ft E–W × 66 ft N–S site with 30 ft roads south and west. Both homes have north-facing doors reached independently from a shared west-side stair/lift. Exact core placement is a design variable; a NW option may be tested while preserving the SW master zone.

Prefer usable east/north openness, north balconies and east garden-facing space. Site gates must connect to the south/west roads; their exact positions are not fixed. Keep pedestrian and vehicle movement clear of each other. Do not import old Property 1 gate positions or generic future-plot entrance preferences.

## Mallanna room

Provide a separate NE room with:

- clear worship space for four people beyond shrine/offerings;
- localized raised platform and table-like surface;
- four decorated pandal pillars (ritual elements, not structural columns);
- prasadam space in front;
- ventilation suitable for lamps/incense;
- storage and a clear entry.

The family or Mallanna/Oggu pujari must confirm deity/image dimensions/facing, platform/pillar/canopy geometry and whether four people includes the pujari.

## Architecture quality

- Prefer realistic room targets, not oversized rectangles that consume spare land.
- Short, clear circulation; avoid hotel-style corridors.
- Test doors, furniture delivery and room clearances.
- Give every habitable room useful daylight and ventilation.
- Keep parking, pedestrian and stair movement independent.
- Consider west heat with overhangs, fins/jaali, verandah and planting.
- Stack kitchens/bathrooms where practical.
- Integrate structural bays/columns early enough to avoid major redesign.
- Preserve useful garden/open space instead of maximizing footprint.
- Follow generic rules on the room designs and interior designs for India.

## Design outputs

For each serious option produce:

1. North-up site plan with roads, gates, surveyed/provisional setbacks and footprint.
2. Dimensioned open-ground, first-floor and second-floor plans from one editable master model.
3. Room/area, door/window and access schedules.
4. Furniture-clearance and parking movement check.
5. Vaastu matrix with PASS / PREFERENCE / CONSULT status.
6. Structural-grid and service-stacking concept.
7. Roof/terrace plan including water tanks and solar panels (do not indicate bore points or water drain systems).
8. Explicit assumptions, conflicts and owner decisions.
9. After plan approval only: realistic plans, cutaways, exterior and room images.

## Decision hierarchy

1. Legal and life safety.
2. Structure, drainage, services and accessibility.
3. Daily usability, privacy, circulation, furniture and climate.
4. Owner-fixed requirements.
5. Vaastu preferences and consultant decisions.
6. Exterior/interior style.

No AI output may claim municipal, structural or final Vaastu certification.

## 5. Area and fit strategy

Use family target ranges; the initial comparison room budgets are L1 918 sq ft including proposed daily pooja and L2 975 sq ft (R1 comparison, not the updated P2 owner-floor budget). These exclude additional circulation, walls/shafts, core and NE open space and do not establish feasibility on 2,397 sq ft of land. Statutory envelope and parking may be more restrictive than area totals suggest.

Start by removing optional family lounge, wraparound balconies and oversized lobby proposals. Then compare compliant room arrangements and envelopes. Do not merge the two owner shrines, lose a bedroom/office, close the NE space or shrink circulation silently. If the complete programme cannot fit, report measured shortfall and owner-visible alternatives; no automatic change to parking count or floor roles.

No exact room coordinates, structural grid, setbacks, balcony widths, lift capacity or floor heights are selected. The next layout must supply clear room polygons/dimensions, actual openings, furniture/use clearances, service gap, core section and parking movement.

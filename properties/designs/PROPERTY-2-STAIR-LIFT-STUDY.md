## 2. Regulatory basis and its limits

The official [BuildNow explanation](https://buildnow.telangana.gov.in/about-us/) identifies the 2012 building rules together with subsequent amendments as its regulatory basis. Check the [current GO register](https://buildnow.telangana.gov.in/go-and-act/) when preparing the application; the original order alone is not a consolidated current permission decision.

In the official [GO 168 compilation](https://tgbpass.telangana.gov.in/html/docs/go_s_and_act/1_GOMsNo_168_MA_UD_dt_07042012.PDF), rule 6(b), PDF page 12, the base non-high-rise rule requires setbacks to remain clear from balcony/corridor edges. This is directly relevant to the proposed north approach and east utility balcony. Verify applicable amendments, exceptions and site conditions before fixing either footprint. No setback values or exemption for an external stair/lift are established by this study.

Design requirement: include stairs, lift shaft, landings, access galleries, walls and projections when assessing the full building against the permitted envelope. **Outside a private dwelling does not mean outside the permitted building footprint.**

## 3. Why the old arrangement cannot be accepted

The existing concept reserves 8 ft (2,438.4 mm) west of the main rectangle while placing a 2,400 mm-wide core there. Only 38.4 mm remains to the assumed boundary. This is an arithmetic clash with the claimed clear west offset, not a compliant site design. The old symbolic stair also does not establish a full-height stair section, headroom or supplier lift fit. See the [repository review](../../reviews/2026-09-27-DESIGN-REVIEW.md).

The owner's NW Bedroom 2, north office and north-facing windows are confirmed design intent. A NW core or north gallery may obstruct them. The west bathroom band also competes with a west-central core. No option may silently move these rooms, lose their required windows or add a living–dining divider to solve the conflict.

## 4A. NW trial design instructions

- Place the shared stair/lift group toward the NW of the full building arrangement, outside both private homes but accounted for in the building envelope. No new setback values are assumed.
- Test common access from the west side of the site to this group, then a landing/approach on the north side of each dwelling and its north-facing main door. Gate position and corridor coverage are proposals, not approved details.
- Keep the dwelling's north-room sequence Bedroom 2 → office → NE living. NW of the entire building and NW within the private dwelling are different references; draw both outlines so this distinction cannot conceal a room move.
- A common route across the north frontage conflicts with the desired direct north windows/privacy. First test a recessed entry/short landing that minimizes passage in front of them. If a longer gallery is unavoidable, show its actual width, cover, window separation and daylight consequences before seeking approval. Do not call the conflict solved by adding a screen or relabelling a window.
- Keep stair/lift arrival outside locked homes on both residential levels, with a continuous common route to ground. Check both floors together; the brother's third bedroom must fit too.
- Owner confirms a **four-person lift** for the NW trial. Supplier-rated load, cabin, door, shaft and landing dimensions remain unresolved. This capacity choice does not establish wheelchair access. Ground and both residential floors are the three required stops. Owner confirms **no terrace lift stop**; the shared staircase continues to the terrace.

Trial result so far: the access relationships are defined, but the north-frontage conflict remains open. No dimensioned fit or feasibility pass has been established.

## 5. Civil information required before freezing the core

1. **Survey and envelope:** true north, boundary lengths, road edges/levels, widening/corner requirements, setbacks, permitted height and projections. Produce a dimensioned full-site plan rather than a dwelling rectangle with residual margins.
2. **Levels and staircase section:** choose floor-to-floor heights and roof access; calculate equal risers, tread goings, clear flight/landing widths, handrails, headroom and beam/slab clearances. Identify the applicable occupancy and adopted stair requirements. Do not assign an arbitrary number of drawn steps. Show arrival and departure at every level, including ground.
3. **Lift brief and supplier drawing:** use the owner-confirmed four-person capacity; use three stops (ground and both homes), with no terrace stop; resolve detailed mobility fit, door clear opening and facing, cabin, shaft construction, pit, overhead, power, rescue and maintenance access. Obtain the supplier's project-specific general arrangement and load data before shaft/foundation design. A passenger count alone does not prove wheelchair access.
4. **Structure:** the structural engineer must coordinate foundations, lift pit, stair supports and the lateral-load system with the open ground floor, room spans, tank and balcony loads. Do not reuse the unverified column count/sizes. Do not add a living–dining wall to hide a structural mismatch.
5. **Daily use and services:** demonstrate separate home locks, safe common escape to the site, door swings, privacy at north windows, parking movement, daylight, bathroom ventilation, waterproofing and maintainable service routes. Assess fire protection and accessibility under the applicable requirements.

Manufacturer evidence illustrates why the lift must be selected rather than guessed: the current [KONE India Villa 100 brochure](https://www.kone.in/Images/kone-villa-100-brochure_tcm152-140345.pdf), PDF pages 8–9, distinguishes shaft and cabin dimensions and specifies model-specific pit and overhead space. It marks the information as planning guidance subject to change. This is an example of the necessary technical information, **not selection or confirmation that this home-lift product suits a shared two-household building**.

The previous 180–260 sq ft core/landing allowance is only a budget placeholder. Replace it with measured stair + shaft + walls + landing + any separate gallery areas; do not force the system to fit that allowance.

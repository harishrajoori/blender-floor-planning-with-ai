## 4. Access and privacy

F-ACCESS-01 (FIXED): shared stair and lift independently reached from the site; both households separately lockable, either reachable while the other is locked. The core is outside each private dwelling, but still part of the total development footprint. For Property 2, the owner confirms a **four-person lift** in the NW trial core; shaft/cabin/door dimensions and rated load require the selected supplier drawing. The lift serves ground and both residential floors only; no roof stop. Terrace access is by the shared staircase. Passenger capacity does not establish wheelchair fit.

F-ACCESS-02 (FIXED, Property 2): each main door is on the north-facing dwelling boundary, with outward normal north. West-side core grouping is the agreed direction; NW is a candidate, not a fixed footprint. Prefer a north entrance toward the east portion only if it connects directly to the hall and preserves the NE open area. Exact pada is HOLD.

F-ACCESS-03 (FIXED): every bedroom, office, common bath and pooja room has ordinary access from living/common circulation. On the owner's floor, the attached bath is immediately north of the SW master bedroom and is entered only from that bedroom; the SW corner remains for sleeping. Master baths on other floors retain private access from their respective bedrooms. Neither a bedroom nor a balcony is the only route to another required indoor room.

F-ACCESS-04: on the owner floor, common bath is **on the west side north of the master attached bath**, entered from a **short shared internal lobby near Bedroom 2/office**, with doorway screened from living/dining (owner-confirmed). It remains common, not an ensuite. Target door-to-door walking route ≤15 ft with ≤2 simple turns. Actual screening/sightlines need geometry checks; do not introduce a living–dining divider or long gallery to obtain privacy.

F-ACCESS-05 (TARGET): weather-protect the common arrival path. A common promenade must not provide uncontrolled entry through bedroom balcony doors. Distinguish shared arrival circulation from private balconies.

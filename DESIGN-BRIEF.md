# Karimnagar family home — owner design brief

Revision: SPEC-2026-09-27-R18, 27 September 2026. Status: reconciled requirements for concept design; dimensions and geometry are not frozen.

Active site: [Property 2, 54 ft E–W × 66 ft N–S](properties/PROPERTY-2-CORNER-WEST-SOUTH-54x66.md), roads west and south 30 ft each. Owner-confirmed dimensions are not a survey. Read the [extended design](properties/designs/PROPERTY-2-DESIGN.md) for arrangement and outstanding decisions.

## Required outcome

Design an open ground level for one car, two bikes, garden/play and temporary family functions; an independent **3BHK for the brother on the first floor**; and **two bedrooms plus office for the owner on the second floor**. A shared stair and owner-confirmed **four-person lift** serve separately lockable homes. Lift serves ground and both residential floors only; terrace access is by the shared staircase. Lift dimensions/model remain unresolved. Both Property 2 home entrances face north, approached from a shared NW stair/lift candidate without crossing either home. The owner selected NW as the first option to test; its dimensions and approach remain unresolved.

Both homes have **one open living–dining space with no dividing wall or TV partition**. The **north-east stays open and connects directly to living/hall**. On the owner's floor, NE is an indoor living extension. Along the east side after it come Mallanna, adjoining daily pooja with a shared wall, the utility passage, then the open SE kitchen. See the [agreed placements](properties/designs/PROPERTY-2-PLACEMENT-DECISIONS.md). This supersedes the earlier requirement to place both shrines in NE.

The owner's SE kitchen has its cooking counter on the east wall, cook facing east, and west side open toward dining. Immediately south of daily pooja, a **4 ft clear-wide passage runs east from dining to utility**, with the open kitchen south of it and cooking fixtures kept out of the passage. Both pooja rooms remain enclosed and share a wall. Mallanna has a west door and north-facing deity; daily pooja has a west door and west-facing deity. Mallanna is confirmed as 7 ft E–W width × at least 9 ft N–S length; daily pooja is confirmed 7 ft E–W × 4 ft N–S. Both have the same E–W span and share an E–W wall. These are owner-confirmed clear internal dimensions between finished wall faces; wall and finish thickness must be added separately.

## Owner's second floor

- SW master sleeping room with cupboards; attached bathroom immediately north, entered only from the master bedroom. Keep the SW corner for sleeping; no required dressing room.
- Bedroom 2 in NW, immediately west of the office, with north-facing window and separate door from common indoor circulation; latest owner-requested swap.
- Personal home office on the north side immediately east of Bedroom 2, with north-facing window and door from short common internal circulation; no through-bedroom route or client entrance. NE living extension remains farther east.
- Open living/dining/kitchen; covered ventilated east utility balcony with washing machine and sink, reached from the 4 ft passage.
- Common bathroom on the west side north of the master attached bath, entered from a short shared internal lobby near Bedroom 2 and office; doorway screened from living/dining.
- Separate daily pooja and Mallanna rooms, independently accessible.
- Mallanna: raised platform/table-like surface, four decorative pandal pillars, prasadam in front and four-person clear worship capacity beyond shrine/offerings.
- No pooja/bath shared wall, opposing doorway or conflicting vertical stack.

## Brother's first floor

Three bedrooms, open living/dining, separate kitchen/utility, common bath and master attached bath. One daily pooja is the current proposal. A second shrine, duplicate office, separate family lounge or full wraparound balcony is not automatically required. Coordinate the three-bedroom plan with upper structure, services and pooja separation.

## Design priorities

Preserve fixed owner decisions while respecting legal/safety constraints. Resolve conflicts explicitly. Test circulation, furniture, daylight, ventilation, privacy, parking and core fit before judging visual style. Maintain useful N/E open space and address west heat. The revised central shrine proposal needs Vaasthu review; a NE label, a grid or a source preference cannot certify it.

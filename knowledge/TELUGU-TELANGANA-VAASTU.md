# Telugu / Telangana Vaasthu implementation checklist

Revision: SPEC-2026-09-27-R1. This compact checklist replaces the older operational guide, whose historical copy is preserved in the [review archive](../reviews/archive/2026-09-27-before-spec-review/knowledge/TELUGU-TELANGANA-VAASTU.md).

Use the [current Vaasthu profile](VAASTU-RULE-PROFILE.md) for active rules, the [family rules](../requirements/COMMON-DESIGN-RULES.md) for owner requirements and the [historical research](VAASTU-KNOWLEDGE.md) for attributed source notes. Research cannot override the latest owner decision.

- [ ] Actual north/reference outline and zone method are recorded; survey uncertainty is visible.
- [ ] Master SW and kitchen SE are checked from footprints and activities, not labels.
- [ ] NE remains open with a direct living/hall connection.
- [ ] Inward/central daily and Mallanna rooms follow the revised owner direction; centre-zone trade-off is recorded for review.
- [ ] Living and dining have no partition or fixed divider.
- [ ] Service passage between kitchen and pooja leads to utility without crossing cooking work space.
- [ ] Pooja rooms are separate and individually accessible; no bathroom adjacency/opposing door/vertical overlap.
- [ ] Four-person Mallanna arrangement, shrine, four pillars, offerings and entry are drawn without conflicting use areas.
- [ ] Owner-floor deity facings are fixed: Mallanna north, daily west, both west doors. Exact entrance/toilet/stair subzones remain HOLD.
- [ ] Each household has independent access; room count matches its programme.
- [ ] Generic geometry, furniture, daylight, structure and legal checks are completed separately.

No automatic rule may merge the two shrines, remove bedrooms, reduce parking or relocate fixed functions to make a compact plot fit. Report alternatives and seek a recorded owner decision on programme changes. A checklist tick must refer to evidence and a revision; these boxes intentionally start unchecked.

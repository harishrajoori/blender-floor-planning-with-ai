## 3. Reconciliation of the current design

1. Reserve owner-floor NE as indoor living extension, not a terrace, with continuous hall connection.
2. Keep living and dining one room without a divider.
3. Along the east side after NE living, fit Mallanna then daily pooja as separate enclosed rooms sharing a wall, both west doors.
4. Immediately south of daily pooja, run the owner-confirmed 4 ft clear passage east from dining to utility, with the open SE kitchen south of it.
5. Overlay the selected centre/NE zones and report the room/altar intersections, including any centre occupation.
6. Record consultant/family acceptance or unresolved conflict. If the selected interpretation rejects the arrangement, present alternatives to the owner; do not silently move pooja back into NE.

Daily pooja being near the kitchen is not automatically a defect. A mandatory 2 ft sealed cavity is not required. Latest owner choices fix deity facings and room targets: Mallanna 7 ft E–W width × at least 9 ft N–S length, deity north; daily 7 ft E–W × 4 ft N–S, deity west; both west doors. Both room axes are confirmed with the same 7 ft E–W span. Finished-inside dimensions are owner-confirmed; detailed ritual fit remains unresolved. The proposed passage has a functional purpose and must meet circulation needs, independently of Vaasthu interpretation.

## 4. How to evaluate a directional claim

Store reference outline, centre, north angle, zone boundaries and rule method. Report how much of a room and its relevant activity footprint lies in each zone. Use a provisional 3×3 overlay only for discussion; no selected occupancy-percentage threshold currently certifies a zone. Exact entrance padas and sensitive NE subzones require a named method.

Check room location, door wall position, outward door facing, person facing and sleeping head direction independently. For example, a deity facing east normally has a facing worshipper looking west. An east-facing cook stands west of the hob. Gate road direction does not determine household door facing.

Possible results are PASS for a precisely scoped check, FAIL, HOLD, NOT_CHECKED or reasoned NOT_APPLICABLE. No plot presently has final Vaasthu acceptance in this repository.
